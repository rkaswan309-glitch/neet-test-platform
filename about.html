About us

NEET Practice Tests is built for one reason: NEET aspirants deserve exam-accurate practice, regardless of what they can afford.

Our goal

Our target is simple — to provide the most affordable, accessible NEET preparation to every student, no matter their background. Practice tests in the real exam format should never be out of reach because of cost.

What we offer

  • Chapter-wise & full-length mock tests in the real NEET CBT format — same layout, timer and question palette as the actual exam.
  • Instant scoring with the real NEET marking scheme, including negative marking.
  • Topic-wise analysis after every attempt, so you know exactly which chapters need more work.
  • Your full test history saved to your account, so you can track your progress over time.
